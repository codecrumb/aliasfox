AliasFox manages your iCloud Hide My Email addresses

A fast, keyboard-first web app for the Hide My Email addresses in your iCloud account. You connect your Apple account once; AliasFox then talks to iCloud for you.

{{SIGNUPS}} Log in

What it does

  • SearchFind any address by label, note, site or status as you type.
  • GenerateCreate a new Hide My Email address and give it a label.
  • LabelEdit labels and notes so you remember where each address is used.
  • Deactivate or deleteStop mail to an address, turn it back on, or delete it.

Everything has a keyboard shortcut: the search box always has focus, Enter copies the selected address, and ? lists the rest. It also works on a phone.

Before you sign up

  • AliasFox is unofficial. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
  • To reach iCloud, the server keeps your Apple web session, encrypted. That session can do more in iCloud than manage Hide My Email. The privacy policy says exactly what is stored and what the risks are.
  • It is provided without warranty, with no guarantee of security or availability. Apple could limit or block access at any time.
